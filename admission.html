The Journey

Our Admission Process

Six thoughtful steps designed to ensure your child finds the right home at Kindergarden. We guide you every step of the way.

01
Step 1

Explore Our Programs & Book a Call

Your journey starts with curiosity. Browse our Montessori, Cambridge, and IB PYP programs online, then schedule a free 20-minute consultation call with our Admissions team. We'll answer your questions and help identify which program best matches your child's learning style and your family's values.

  • Review program overviews on our Programs page
  • Download the current Academic Year Prospectus
  • Book a free 20-min consultation via phone or Zoom
  • Receive a personalised program recommendation
Anytime — no appointment needed
02
Step 2

Schedule a Campus Visit & Open Day

Experience Kindergarden firsthand. Book a personalised campus tour guided by our friendly Admissions counsellor. You and your child will explore classrooms, outdoor play areas, the library, and creative labs. We also host monthly Open Day events where you can meet teachers, see lessons in action, and connect with current parents.

  • Guided 60-minute campus walkthrough
  • Sit in on a live classroom session
  • Meet the Head of School and key teachers
  • Monthly Open Days every 2nd Saturday
1 – 2 hours · Book 3 days in advance
03
Step 3

Submit Your Application Online

Ready to apply? Complete our secure online application form. We've designed it to be simple, fast, and mobile-friendly — most families finish in under 15 minutes. Along with the form, upload the required documents and pay the non-refundable application processing fee of S$80.

  • Fill in the child's personal and medical details
  • Upload birth certificate & previous school records
  • Submit a parent statement (optional but recommended)
  • Pay the S$80 application processing fee
  • Receive instant email confirmation of submission
~15 minutes · Available 24/7 online
04
Step 4

Child Readiness Assessment

This is a relaxed, play-based activity session — not a formal exam. Our experienced teachers invite your child to spend 45 minutes in a classroom environment, engaging in age-appropriate activities. The goal is to understand your child's learning style, curiosity, and social readiness so we can place them in the best possible class and programme.

  • Play-based, stress-free activity session
  • Language, numeracy & creative skills observed
  • Social and emotional wellbeing noted
  • Parents receive a brief written report
45 minutes · On-campus
05
Step 5

Receive Your Offer & Acceptance

Within 5 working days of the assessment, our Admissions team will send you a formal offer letter by email and post. The offer letter includes your child's assigned program, class, and the start date. You will have 10 business days to accept the offer and secure your child's place.

  • Offer letter issued within 5 working days
  • Review the programme, class, and fees schedule
  • Sign and return the Acceptance Form digitally
  • Pay the first-month deposit to secure the seat
Accept within 10 business days
06
Step 6

Welcome to Kindergarden — Your First Day!

Congratulations — your child is officially part of the Kindergarden family! Before Day 1, you'll receive a Welcome Pack with a school calendar, uniform guidelines, meal plans, and your Parent Portal login. Our "Gentle Start" induction program helps new children transition comfortably into their new environment over the first two weeks.

  • Receive the Digital Welcome Pack & school handbook
  • Access the Parent Portal for updates & communications
  • Two-week Gentle Start transition programme
  • Meet your dedicated class teacher and pastoral tutor
  • Join the Parent Community WhatsApp group
The beginning of something wonderful!
Eligibility & Documents

Who Can Apply?

Kindergarden welcomes children of all nationalities and backgrounds. We evaluate each child individually, with a focus on holistic fit rather than academic ability alone.

🎓 Program Age Eligibility

  • Nursery (Montessori): 2 – 3 years
  • Pre-K (Montessori / Cambridge): 3 – 4 years
  • Kindergarten 1 & 2: 4 – 6 years
  • Primary 1 – 6 (Cambridge / IB PYP): 6 – 12 years
  • Age determined by birth date as of 1 January of the academic year

📄 Required Documents

  • Child's original birth certificate (with certified copy)
  • Passport-sized photograph (recent, colour)
  • Immunisation / vaccination records
  • Most recent school report card (if applicable)
  • Copy of parent/guardian passport or NRIC
  • Special needs or medical documentation (if applicable)

🌍 International Applicants

  • All nationalities are warmly welcomed
  • Dependent Pass or Student Pass required for non-citizens
  • English proficiency support available for EAL learners
  • Mid-year enrolment considered based on availability

📅 Important Dates

  • Academic year begins: January & July intakes
  • Application portal opens: 3 months before intake
  • Priority deadline (siblings): 6 weeks before intake
  • Open Days: Every 2nd Saturday of the month
  • Enrolment confirmation: Rolling basis
